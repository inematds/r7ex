In short

r7ex is INEMA’s vertical reel production line (9:16). You bring a strong idea, the thesis, and it puts together a video about 40 to 46 seconds long (always under 1 minute) with Nei’s avatar speaking, images generated by Codex, and a TV-news finish: a title at the opening, panels that appear on the right word, and a counter and captions that light up along with the speech. The script follows 7 trigger stages, from the hook (one of 5 types, with the headline already written in the first frame) to the request for a comment, always guiding people to inema.club. To use it, you need Python, Node, Codex, and a HeyGen subscription, running on INEMA’s machine.

What it is

The r7e script with the TV-news finish

r7ex = r7e + x. r7e (reel7etapas) provides the 7-stage script; the x comes from eventosextremospub, the TV-news finish that went the most viral. The repo works on its own, with its own copy of everything.

🎯 Plan before speaking

Audience, what the person wants, a hook of one of the 5 types with a number and source (never a list), potential score (below 8, change the angle), and the inema.club page that solves it. One topic per video.

📺 TV-news look

Title drawn in the opening, black and yellow, a banner with a red badge, panels and counters timed to the speech, and karaoke captions.

🧩 Standalone

Render engine, HeyGen studio, transcription, music track, bot delivery and queue copied into the repo. Changing things here doesn't affect the other projects.

The 7 stages

Real example: "Hackers stole the keys"

The order of stages 2 to 6 can adapt to the topic (in the reel "Who makes money in 2027" stage 3 became proof), but there are always 7 parts.

How it works

The engine doesn't create content: it syncs and animates

The engine is a web page (motor/mapa.html) filmed at 30 frames per second. It receives the avatar, the timing of each word, the images and the scene generated from the sheet.

Thesis + destination→ Plan and sheet→ Avatar on HeyGen→ Codex images→ Word timing (Whisper)→ Pacing (~180 words/min + card)→ Scene→ Render→ Review sheet→ Bot v3 + channel queue
1

Layers per frame

Black background, the current image with a light zoom, top banner with badge, panel with items and counter, karaoke captions and the CTA as a question.

2

Filming

motor/render.mjs records the page in a hidden Chrome (display :99), without opening anything on the screen of whoever is working.

3

Compositing

motor/compor_avatar.sh puts the face in the corner box, the voice at −14 LUFS and the low music track, which ducks when the voice comes in, falls silent during the pause before the punchline and continues during the final card.

Prerequisites

What needs to be on the machine

r7ex was built for the INEMA server. Copies on another machine need the same pieces.

Python + Node

Python 3 for the scripts and local Whisper (the inemavox one); Node for the render engine and the HeyGen studio; ffmpeg for pacing and compositing; Claude Code (claude -p) for the plan score.

npm install   # engine dependencies

Codex CLI

Draws the opening with the title and one image per part, through the subscription.

codex --version

HeyGen (studio)

Nei's avatar through the subscription, in the hidden browser. Generate only with the OK for that production.

# Nei's OK for this reel
mkdir -p edicoes/<pasta>/avatar
echo 'Nei, <data>: "ok"' > edicoes/<pasta>/avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN
Usage guide · step by step

From request to reel in the queue

A one-line request is enough: "r7ex: <your thesis> · destination: <course/page> · channel: lives7 · you may generate the avatar". After that, these are the commands.

1

Create the edition

Copies the template into a new folder with the sheet to fill in: plan, opening, the 7 parts (speech + image or screenshot + panel), CTA and destination.

python3 r7ex.py novo hacker   # creates edicoes/<data>-hacker/ficha.yaml
2

Pass the sheet gate

Check 7 parts, speech between 450 and 620 characters (a topic with many items: up to 800, with fala_max and fala_max_motivo in the sheet), whether every panel word appears in that part’s speech, and the gancho block: valid type, headline of up to 6 words that differs from the speech, proof by ~20 s, and absolution. List hooks fail. Then plano scores the script: it needs a hook ≥ 8 and an average ≥ 7.5 before recording the avatar.

python3 r7ex.py checar edicoes/<data>-hacker
python3 r7ex.py plano edicoes/<data>-hacker   # plan score
3

Do everything at once

Images + avatar → word timing → pacing (stretched speech, pause before the punchline, 9 s card) → scene → render → 12-frame sheet. With --de it reuses a ready-made avatar.

python3 r7ex.py fazer edicoes/<data>-hacker
python3 r7ex.py fazer edicoes/<data>-hacker --de avatar.mp4   # avatar already exists
4

Review before sending

Read the opening (title spelling) and the review sheet: nothing covering anything, legible screenshots.

# open and check
edicoes/<pasta>/capa/abertura.png
edicoes/<pasta>/build/folha.jpg
5

Send and publish through the queue

Sends the video to bot v3 and puts it at the end of the channel queue (default lives7). The queue publishes 1 every 2 h, from 9 am to 9 pm. Never a direct upload.

python3 r7ex.py enviar edicoes/<pasta>   # bot v3
python3 r7ex.py fila edicoes/<pasta>     # end of the channel queue
python3 fila/fila.py --status          # view the queue

Separate steps: avatar, imagens, cena, render, revisar (run python3 r7ex.py to see them all).

What’s new · v1.2.0 · 28/10/2026

The hook is decided in the first 3 seconds

We compared r7ex reels with reels that hold attention: they used the same format, but not the same hook techniques. The 28/10 edit opened with "5 AI updates…", a list hook that none of the reference reels use. Version 1.2.0 treats the first frame as the video cover.

Hook typeWhat it is
contrario
PARA DE CORRIGIR IA NO CHAT
(stop correcting AI in the chat)		tells you to stop or turns common sense upside down
perda
E SE SEU CHAT SUMISSE AMANHÃ?
(what if your chat disappeared tomorrow?)		hypothetical loss
estatistica
METADE DO CÓDIGO É LIXO
(half the code is junk)		a scary number, with a verified source
terceiro
ACORDOU DEVENDO US$ 1.700
(woke up owing US$1,700)		someone else’s story, with a number
noticia
SAIU O CARRO VOADOR?
(did the flying car come out?)		this week’s news, with a date and source

The type names (contrario, perda, estatistica, terceiro, noticia) are the literal values of gancho.tipo in the sheet. The headlines stay in Portuguese, as they appear in the video; the translation goes below.

1

Headline stronger than the speech

The headline is already written in the first frame: up to 6 words, in uppercase, with one word in yellow. It says it more forcefully than the voice: the screen says "PARA DE CORRIGIR" (stop correcting), and the voice says "I don't think you should". Lists ("5 updates…"), "did you know…" and "I made…" are rejected by checar.

2

Damaged object and a change every 1 s

A broken or threatened object appears in the center of the cover, such as a trash can, a broken key, or a red scratch. In the first 3 seconds, the image changes framing every second: the cover at 0 s and two zooms, at 1 s and 2 s, without removing the headline from the screen.

3

Proof, absolution, and one topic

The sourced number appears by 20 seconds. Right after that comes the viewer’s problem, without blame ("it’s not that you haven’t studied; nobody gave you the map"). Each video covers just one topic: if the request includes several, the sheet proposes one video for each.

gancho:
  tipo: terceiro
  manchete: "ACORDOU DEVENDO US$ 1.700"   # up to 6 words, uppercase
  destaque: "1.700"                       # the word in yellow
  objeto: "chave dourada partida, com um risco vermelho"
  prova: {parte: 1, de: "1.700"}          # sourced number by ~20 s
  absolvicao: "não é descuido seu"        # same as in the speech in part 2 or 3
  fontes: [{nome: "relatos de usuários, out/2026", trecho: "US$ 1.700 numa noite"}]
What’s new · v1.1.0 · 28/10/2026

More time to read, listen, and comment

We measured the older edits: speech raced along at 202–222 words per minute, without a single breath, and the comment card stayed on screen for less than 2 seconds because the video cut off on the last word. Version 1.1.0 fixes both issues.

1

Card held for 9 s

The video no longer cuts off on the last word. The comment card stays on screen for about 9 seconds and shows the action: the "Add a comment…" field, text being typed, the Post button being pressed, and the comment being posted. The avatar stays still in the corner, and the music continues to the end.

2

Speech at ~180 words/min

HeyGen speaks at about 200–215 words per minute. The edit stretches the speech by up to 12%, without changing the tone and with the mouth still in sync, to get close to 180. Before the punchline, there’s a pause of 0.5 to 0.9 seconds, with the music silenced.

3

Shorter script

Speech is now 450 to 620 characters, about 30 to 37 seconds at the new pace. A list reel can go up to 800 characters, with the reason written in the brief (fala_max and fala_max_motivo), and stays under 1 minute with the card.
